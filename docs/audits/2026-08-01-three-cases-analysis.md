## Case 1 — the rally was a liquidity-vacuum snapback, not buying

At the low (28487.00, 09:33:49.7): first +10.75 pts retraced on **delta +2** (63 buy vs 61 sell) — nobody bought the low; selling stopped and the same emptiness cut both ways. Real initiative buying only from +6 s. At the low tick the bid side was THINNER than the ask (top-5: 8 vs 15) — the bottom was not defended. The only true absorption (hidden iceberg at 28500.00, 80 c on displayed ≤1) **failed** — price ran 13 pts through it.

**Dead metrics (measured, not opinion):** pulled-vs-eaten % (76% on the final leg vs 85% baseline — constant everywhere), aggregate top-5 depth (flat ~12 lots in every phase), ask thinning on the rally (never happened). T2 reproduces here (0.46 REVERSAL); **T1 does NOT reproduce on NQ** (max print 5 = top 0.51% — NQ's print-size tail is nothing like MNQ's); T3 is degenerate at NQ scale (its −200/−250 arms occur 0.018%/never).

**Scan verdict (frozen FLUSH→BOUNCE→HIGHER-LOW→RECLAIM rule, whole session): NOT tradeable as specified.** 17 firings, 5 winners, negative R at every exit horizon, indistinguishable from random-time long entry with the same trade management (p = 0.155), and it *underperforms* a random second on raw forward move. It did mark the day's 3 real bottoms (09:33, 10:43, 11:28) — keep the structure as **entry timing** for a position that has an independent reason to exist, never as the reason. 81% of its arms get taken out within 180 s on a trend day; it is a downtrend metronome. Also: 11/11 stop-outs traded through the stop (up to 5.75 pt) — "clean −1R losers" is false at 1 s resolution.

## Cross-cutting findings (the ones that change the project)

1. **Reversal vs continuation is distinguishable at t_end+0 ms** on this session's evidence: *lumpy, rare-max-print sweep into virgin prices with starving supply inside a trend leg* → reversal-prone (R, case 1); *uniform, many-peer sweep through a TESTED balance low* → continuation-prone (case 3). Sweep anatomy + structural context; no 5–10 s confirmation metric survived (T3 degenerate, D5 defeated).
2. **INSTRUMENT BUG — corpus-invalidating if ignored.** The audit's thresholds were calibrated on MNQ; the same market moment measures 41.6 c/ms on MNQ vs 0.95 c/ms on NQ (44×). T1's ≥15 c/ms arm is unreachable on NQ (session max 2.86); contract-denominated constants (−200/−250) don't transfer. **Record and score the instrument you trade (NQ), or re-register thresholds per instrument.** The old manual recordings are MNQ; the 08-01 auto captures are NQ.
3. **The displayed book carries almost nothing** on this feed: depth flat, pulls constant, big sizes non-predictive, defended-looking levels mark failure points. The two survivors worth watching: the *re-offered exact-tick ceiling* before a second test (case 2), and per-level extension volume (T2) which fired correctly in both its evaluable cases.
4. **Outcome-label defect (again):** §4's recovery label can score a −28.5 pt trade as a "correct REVERSAL" (it measures recovery from the extension low, not from signal price) and my literal implementation labels the audit's own C event REVERSAL. The label needs an MAE-from-signal term and a pinned recovery definition before gating anything.
5. **Corpus economics:** each of the three signatures fires ~1×/session ⇒ ~30 sessions each under the frozen gate. The ladder-covariate route (case 2) is the only one with tractable economics (2–3 sessions). Also: at the shipped MinVolume=150 the case-3 signal does not exist — **the corpus must record at MinVolume=50**; and record range days, not just liquidation days (65% of this session sat above K1's ceiling).
